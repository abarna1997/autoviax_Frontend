# AutoViaX Horizon

Build a premium, fully responsive dark-theme marketing website for AutoViaX.net, an Autonomous Mobility Intelligence platform for automotive and logistics organizations. Create Home, About, Product, Contact, Privacy Policy, and Terms & Conditions pages. The design must feel high-end, futuristic automotive, minimal, and subtly glassmorphic, using #010206, #050709, #D2D2D2, #97CFFF, #EFEFEF. Typography: Satoshi semibold headings and Planc regular/medium/semibold body if available; use close fallbacks if not. Take visual inspiration from https://www.behance.net/gallery/250457381/Voltpark-Luxury-EV-Charging-App-(UXUI-Case-Study)?tracking_source=search_projects|dark+theme+&l=3 without copying it. Ensure each page has a distinct composition and visual storytelling approach; do not reuse the same generic page format.

Home: polished header/navigation, immersive hero with AI mobility visual, AutoViaX positioning, trusted by/partners, differentiated features, live operations dashboard preview, use cases, testimonials, FAQ, strong CTA, footer. Include convincing fleet intelligence visualizations, route optimization storytelling, environmental awareness, and automotive AI architecture cues. Use refined microinteractions and full-site effects where appropriate without hurting readability.

About: company story, mission and vision with a distinct editorial/scrollytelling design, leadership team, partners.
Contact: distinctive contact layout with contact form, book-a-demo CTA, office locations, and a map representation.
Product: product-led experience explaining autonomous mobility intelligence, data-to-action workflow, modules/capabilities, dashboard visuals, integrations/architecture and CTA.
Privacy and Terms: accessible, well-structured legal pages matching brand but with clearly readable content.

Make navigation and footer functional between all pages. Use strong responsive behavior across desktop, tablet, and mobile. Use polished original imagery/abstract visuals where needed; avoid placeholder-looking sections. No backend needed for this initial marketing build.
